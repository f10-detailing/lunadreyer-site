Common questions

Do you provide NAATI-certified translations?

No. NAATI is what Australian government departments usually require for foreign documents, for example for a visa. Documents leaving Australia are judged by the receiving country's rules, and private and commercial work is your choice. If you need NAATI, we will tell you straight away.

How much will it cost?

Documents for use overseas start from $69 per page including GST, and business translation from $25 per 100 words plus GST. Every job is confirmed in a fixed written quote first. See full pricing.

How long does it take?

Translation usually takes a few business days. A power of attorney with notarisation and apostille typically takes one to three weeks end to end, mostly depending on how quickly the receiving lawyer confirms the wording.

Do Notary and apostille fees come on top?

Yes. The Notary Public and DFAT charge their own fees, which are paid to them. We coordinate the steps and tell you the costs up front.

Which languages do you work in?

German, English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Catalan, plus Greek (including Cypriot Greek), Maltese and French through our collaborators.
