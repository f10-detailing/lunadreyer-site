FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions I hear most often. If yours is not here, get in touch.

Are your translations NAATI certified?

No. NAATI certification applies to some Australian requirements. European requirements vary by country and by office, so the first step is to confirm what the receiving office needs.

How does a document travel from Australia to Europe?

The usual path has five steps: settle the requirements, translate, notarise, apostille, and lodge abroad. Each step is confirmed with you before the next one starts.

Do you handle powers of attorney?

Yes. Powers of attorney are one of the main things I translate and prepare for European use. The power of attorney page explains the steps in order.

Do you work with Italian lawyers who have Australian clients?

Yes. There is an Italian-language page for lawyers, and I work with Australian documents that Italian lawyers need for their own clients.

What if I am not sure what my document needs?

Send me the document and the country it is going to. I will tell you what the receiving office is likely to need before you commit to anything.
