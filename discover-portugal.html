Live the Portuguese dream, by the Atlantic.

Sunshine, the ocean at the end of the street, fresh seafood and pastéis de nata, and an easy pace of life that leaves time for long lunches. Portugal is where many Australians picture their next chapter. These four places show why.

Four places to fall for

The capital

Lisbon

A city of seven hills, yellow trams climbing the lanes of Alfama, and a golden light that rolls in off the Tagus river. The beaches and marina of Cascais and the palaces and forested hills of Sintra are an easy trip away.

The north

Porto

Colourful houses tumbling down to the Ribeira waterfront on the Douro river, and the port lodges of Vila Nova de Gaia just across the bridge. Upstream, the terraced vineyards of the Douro Valley make one of the world's most beautiful wine regions.

The south coast

The Algarve

Golden cliffs, sea caves and sheltered beaches around Lagos and the Benagil coast, with famously sunny weather for most of the year. Championship golf courses, fresh grilled fish and a relaxed village pace make it a favourite place to settle.

The Alentejo coast

Comporta

Long, quiet beaches, rice fields, pine forests and low-key villages, about an hour and a half south of Lisbon. Simple, stylish and close to nature, it is the place to slow right down.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Portugal?

Spring and early autumn bring warm days, blue skies and quieter streets, perfect for Lisbon and Porto. Summer is the season for the Algarve and the Alentejo beaches, and the south stays mild and sunny through winter.

Can you rent in Portugal for a season first?

Yes. Medium-term rentals of a few months are a popular way to settle into a neighbourhood, find a favourite café and see how a town feels across the seasons before choosing where to live.

Where in Portugal is best for the beach?

The Algarve is famous for its golden cliffs and sheltered coves, Comporta and the Alentejo coast for long, quiet sands, and Cascais for city beaches just a train ride from Lisbon.
