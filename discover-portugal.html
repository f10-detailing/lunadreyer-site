Property · Portugal

Live the Portuguese dream, by the Atlantic.

Sunshine, the ocean at the end of the street, fresh seafood and pastéis de nata, and an easy pace of life that leaves time for long lunches. Portugal is where many Australians picture their next chapter. These four places show why, and when you are ready to buy, we handle the Portuguese paperwork from Australia.

Four places to fall for

Lisbon

The capital. A city of seven hills, yellow trams climbing the lanes of Alfama, and a golden light that rolls in off the Tagus river. The beaches and marina of Cascais and the palaces and forested hills of Sintra are an easy trip away.

Porto

The north. Colourful houses tumbling down to the Ribeira waterfront on the Douro river, and the port lodges of Vila Nova de Gaia just across the bridge. Upstream, the terraced vineyards of the Douro Valley make one of the world's most beautiful wine regions.

The Algarve

The south coast. Golden cliffs, sea caves and sheltered beaches around Lagos and the Benagil coast, with famously sunny weather for most of the year. Championship golf courses, fresh grilled fish and a relaxed village pace make it a favourite place to settle.

Comporta

The Alentejo coast. Long, quiet beaches, rice fields, pine forests and low-key villages, about an hour and a half south of Lisbon. Simple, stylish and close to nature, it is the place to slow right down.

Buying in Portugal from Australia

  1. Get your NIF

    The Portuguese tax number you need to buy, pay taxes and connect utilities, available to non-residents. We prepare and translate the documents you need to apply.

  2. Open a Portuguese bank account

    Used to pay the deposit, the taxes and the purchase price. We translate the bank's forms and correspondence so you know exactly what you are signing.

  3. Sign the contrato-promessa

    The contrato-promessa de compra e venda is the promissory contract that secures the property with a deposit. We translate it into English so you understand every clause before you commit.

  4. Sign the procuração in Australia

    A Portuguese power of attorney signed before an Australian Notary Public and apostilled by DFAT, so your representative can sign for you and you do not need to fly over. We prepare it in Portuguese and English. Procuração na Austrália · How the power of attorney and apostille work

  5. Pay IMT and stamp duty

    The property transfer tax (IMT) and stamp duty are paid before the deed is signed. We translate the tax documents and receipts for your records in Australia.

  6. Sign the escritura

    The deed of sale is signed before a notário or through the Casa Pronta service. We translate the escritura into English for you.

  7. Register the property

    The purchase is recorded at the Conservatória do Registo Predial, and the home is officially yours. We translate the registry certificate so you have it in English.

Common questions

Can Australians buy property in Portugal?

Yes. There are no restrictions on foreigners buying property in Portugal. You need a NIF (Portuguese tax number), and we prepare the documents you need to get one from Australia.

Is it necessary to travel to Portugal to buy?

No. With a procuração (power of attorney) signed before an Australian Notary Public and apostilled by DFAT, your representative signs the contrato-promessa and the escritura for you. We prepare the procuração in Portuguese and English.

Where can you see property for sale in Portugal?

The main listing sites are Idealista, Imovirtual and Casa Sapo. Official auctions are published on the e-leilões portal.

Do you translate Portuguese documents?

Yes. We translate contracts, notary deeds, registry certificates and correspondence between Portuguese and English, so you understand every document before you sign.
