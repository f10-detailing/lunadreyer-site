Get in touch

Tell us what you need, and where it is going.

A few details are enough to start: which service you need, which languages or country are involved, and how to reach you. We reply with a fixed quote, and if we are not the right people for it, we will say so rather than take the work on.

What to have ready

A faster, more useful reply usually comes down to a few details being ready from the start.

  • What the document actually is, or what you think it needs to be
  • Which country it is going to, and ideally which notary, registry, court or lawyer is asking for it
  • Your identity details exactly as they appear on your passport, if the matter involves a power of attorney
  • Any deadline you are working to, so we can tell you early if it is realistic

If you do not have all of this yet, write anyway. Working out what is still missing is often half the value of an early email. For prices, see services and pricing.
