Tell us what the document is, and where it is going.

A few details are enough to start: what you need done, which country the document is for, and how to reach you. We will reply with the steps your document needs.

What to have ready

A faster, more useful reply usually comes down to a few details being ready from the start.

  • What the document actually is, or what you think it needs to be
  • Which country it is going to, and ideally which notary, registry, court or lawyer is asking for it
  • Your identity details exactly as they appear on your passport, if the matter involves a power of attorney
  • Any deadline you are working to, so we can plan each step around it

If you do not have all of this yet, write anyway. Working out what is still missing is often half the value of an early email.
