Website building and website translation

A website that works on every phone, in every language your customers speak.

I build clear, fast websites for Melbourne small businesses, set up Shopify online stores as a Shopify Partner, get products into Google Shopping, and translate websites so they read naturally in another language. One person handles the design, the words and the translation, so nothing gets lost between them.

Website building

For businesses whose current site is old, slow, hard to use on a phone, or does not exist yet.

Small business websites

Shopify online stores

Google Shopping and Merchant Center

Getting your products into Google Shopping: Merchant Center setup, product feeds, and fixing the disapproved products and warnings that stop items from showing.

Written for you

Found on Google

Page titles and descriptions set up properly, the site submitted to Google, and help setting up or tidying your Google Business Profile.

Website translation

For businesses that want to reach customers in another language, and for overseas businesses that want an English site that reads as if it were written in English.

Spanish and English, both directions

Translating an English site into Spanish for Melbourne's Spanish-speaking community, or a Spanish site into English.

German, Italian and Portuguese into English

For businesses in Germany, Italy, Portugal or Brazil that need an English version of their website for Australian or international customers.

Multilingual Shopify stores

Product pages, collections, checkout wording and policies translated and set up inside Shopify, so each customer sees the store in their language.

Set up so Google finds each language

Each language version is set up so search engines can show the right one to the right people, rather than a single page with a translate button.

Website translation is localisation: the words are adapted so they read naturally for that audience, not just converted word for word. It is not a certified translation. For documents that need a NAATI-certified translation, see translation services.

Common questions

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. I handle the build, the setup and the text. You tell me about your business and approve the result.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain, hosting and email are registered in your name, and the site is handed over to you on launch.

Can you update my existing site instead of starting again?

Sometimes. If your current site is on a platform that is still supported, a refresh can be quicker and cheaper. I will tell you which makes more sense once I have looked at it.

Can you translate my website and build it at the same time?

Yes. That is the advantage of having one person do both: the design leaves room for each language, and the translated text is written for the page it sits on.

Which languages do you translate websites into?

Spanish and English in both directions, and into English from German, Italian and Portuguese. Ask about other combinations.
