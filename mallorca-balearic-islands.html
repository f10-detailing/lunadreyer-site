Meet our partner in Mallorca

Carmen, on the ground for you

Carmen lives in Mallorca and works for our clients across the Balearic Islands. While you stay in Australia, she is there in person: at the viewing, with the inspector, at the negotiating table and at the notario's office.

Carmen is a native speaker of Mallorquín, the island's own Catalan, so conversations with local owners and sellers are simple and direct, in their own language. She is also a translator in German, Spanish and Catalan (Mallorquín). Together with our team in Melbourne, you have one contact in Australia and one on the island, working on the same file.

Mallorca and the Balearics: common questions

Is it possible to buy a property in Mallorca without travelling from Australia?

Yes. You sign a power of attorney once in Australia, which we prepare, have notarised within the week and apostilled at DFAT. Carmen, our partner in Mallorca, then arranges the inspection, the negotiation and the notario, and your representative signs the escritura for you.

Which islands does Carmen cover?

All of the Balearic Islands: Mallorca, Menorca, Ibiza and Formentera. She lives in Mallorca and travels to the other islands for appointments.

Which languages does Carmen speak?

Carmen is a native speaker of Mallorquín, the Catalan spoken on the island, and a translator in German, Spanish and Catalan. Local owners and sellers talk to her directly in their own language, and our Melbourne team keeps you informed in English.

What happens after the purchase?

Carmen offers concierge help on the island: insurance and utility calls, contact with the community of owners and the practical side of owning a home in Mallorca while you are in Australia.

Found your place in the Balearics?

Tell us the island, the property and your date, and we bring in Carmen from day one.
