Concierge

Everyday matters in Australia, handled in your language.

Calls, letters, appointments and complaints that are hard to manage in English. We act as your go-between with tradespeople, service providers, building managers and councils, and keep you informed at every step.

What we take care of

Repairs and tradespeople

Finding a technician, explaining the fault, booking the visit and translating the quote before you agree to it.

Driving licences and transport offices

Checking which documents are needed for a renewal or an overseas licence conversion, then attending the appointment with you.

Cancelling subscriptions and contracts

Phone, internet, energy, gym and streaming contracts: contacting the provider, requesting the cancellation and getting it confirmed in writing.

Complaints to owners corporations

Putting a building, maintenance or common-property issue in writing to the owners corporation or building manager, and following it up.

Requests to local councils

Preparing and lodging requests and enquiries with your council, from bins and parking to permits and neighbourhood issues.

Appointments and letters

Booking appointments, explaining letters you receive and replying on time, so nothing is missed because of the language.

Common questions

Can you help with medical appointments?

Yes. We find the right practitioner, book the appointment, request the free TIS National phone interpreter for the consultation, and help with the paperwork and instructions before and after.

Which languages do you work in?

Spanish, German, Italian, Catalan and English, and through partner translators Greek, Cypriot Greek, Maltese and other languages.

Can you come along in person?

Yes, in person, by phone or by video call, depending on the task and where you are. The quote says which before anything starts.

When a matter also involves a lawyer or migration agent, we work alongside them and take care of the language side. Información en español.

Something you need sorted?

Tell us what it is, who it involves and when it needs to happen. We reply with what we can do and a fixed quote.
