Power of attorney for Europe: common questions

How long does a power of attorney from Australia take?

Usually one to three weeks from the approval of the text. We book the Australian Notary Public usually within the week, take the signed document to DFAT ourselves for the apostille and courier the original overseas with a scan the same day.

How much does an apostille cost in Australia?

DFAT charges A$105 for each apostille from 1 January 2026. We quote the whole route, drafting, translation, notary booking, apostille and courier, at a fixed price agreed in advance.

Is it possible to buy property in Europe without travelling?

Yes. With a power of attorney signed once in Australia, someone you trust signs the deed for you before the notary overseas. We prepare it to that notary's wording so it is accepted the first time.

Which countries accept an Australian apostille?

Every member of the Hague Apostille Convention, including Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, Cyprus, Malta and Germany. No further legalisation is needed for use in those countries.
