About the agency

A small agency built around one kind of problem.

Luna Dreyer Translation helps people and businesses in Australia get documents, messages and everyday matters understood and accepted in another language, most often by a notary, registry, lawyer or court in Europe. We translate, and we facilitate: we work out what the receiving side needs and coordinate the steps that get it there.

Our founder

Luna Dreyer holds a Bachelor of Arts in Übersetzungswissenschaft (Translation Studies) from Heidelberg University, in German, English and Spanish, and also works in Italian and Portuguese. Heidelberg's translation institute was the first of its kind in Europe, and its training centres on legal and official text, where precision is the whole job.

The agency grew out of that work. Clients who came for one power of attorney kept coming back with the next problem: a Greek property, a Maltese registry, a French bank, a relative who needed someone at an appointment. Bringing in trusted collaborators means we can now take those on with the same standard.

How we work

Requirements first

Before anything is translated, we confirm what the receiving authority, lawyer or institution actually needs. It is the step that prevents most rejections.

Fixed quotes

Published starting prices where the market publishes them, and a fixed written quote before any work begins. See pricing.

Clear limits

We are translators and facilitators, not lawyers, notaries or migration agents, and we do not give legal advice. We do not provide NAATI-certified translations.

Confidentiality

Your documents and details are used only for the job you asked for, and shared only with the people who need them to complete it.

Work with us

Tell us what you need and where it is going. If we are not the right people for it, we will say so.
