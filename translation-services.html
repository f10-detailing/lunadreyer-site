Translation for documents that have to work somewhere else.

Most of our work is Spanish and English, in both directions. We also translate into English from German, Italian, Portuguese and Catalan. The common thread is documents that have to hold up with a specific reader: a registry, a bank, an institution or a court, not a general audience.

Getting the right certificate format

For a birth, marriage or death certificate, many receiving authorities specifically want the international or multilingual version that some issuing registries can produce, rather than a translation of the standard domestic certificate. Ask us before you order the certificate and we will help you work out which format your authority asks for. If the international version is not available, or the authority does not ask for it, we translate the standard certificate.

How a translated qualification is recognised

A translation lets the institution or authority you are applying to read your degree, transcript or school record. Recognition is a separate step that the institution runs itself, such as an equivalence assessment, additional modules or specific coursework. We translate your documents so they are ready for that process, and share what we know about how the institution handles it.

Common questions

Can you check what the receiving authority needs?

Yes. Requirements are set by the office that receives the document, so we confirm what it asks for before any work starts, and prepare the translation to match.

Can you translate a birth, marriage or death certificate?

Yes. Many receiving authorities want the international or multilingual version some registries issue, so we help you check which format your authority asks for first. Where the standard certificate is what you need, we translate it.

Can you translate a degree for an overseas application?

Yes. The translation lets the receiving institution read your degree or transcript, so it is ready for the recognition process the institution runs itself.

How is the price worked out?

By the document: what it is, its length, and any notarisation or apostille steps involved. We quote a fixed price upfront rather than charging by the word.
