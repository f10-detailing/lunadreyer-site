Winter here, summer there

The seasons work in your favour. When the days get short in Melbourne, the Mediterranean is at its best.

June to August

Winter in Australia, high summer in Europe: warm seas, outdoor dinners and daylight until late in the evening.

Festa season

Village feasts, saints' days, fireworks over the harbour and Ferragosto on 15 August across Italy and Greece.

Home for Christmas

Back to the Australian summer for the holidays, so you get the best of both, every year.

Live there longer

Three countries offer residence to people who buy a home there, so a summer house can become a second home for as long as you like.

Greece, from €400,000

A five-year golden visa, renewable, for a home of at least 120 m². €800,000 in Athens, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini and the larger islands.

Cyprus, from €300,000

Permanent residence with a new home from a developer, plus VAT, and a secure income from abroad.

Malta, from €375,000

The Malta Permanent Residence Programme, with a home bought anywhere in Malta or Gozo, or rented.

How buying from Australia works

Common questions

When is the best time for Australians to go?

June to August. It is winter in Melbourne and high summer across southern Europe, with long evenings, warm seas and village festivals. Many Australians stay for the whole season, and spring and autumn are lovely for a quieter stay.

Is it common for Australians to spend every winter in Europe?

Yes. Many retirees and families with ties to Italy, Greece, Malta and Cyprus split the year: summer in Europe while Australia has winter, then home for the Australian summer. Renting for a season or buying a holiday home are both popular.

Which country suits a relaxed retirement?

It depends on the life you picture. Malta and Cyprus are small, English is widely spoken and everything is close. Portugal and Spain combine city life with beaches and good value. Italy and Greece offer village life, food and family roots. Each country page shows the places we love most.
