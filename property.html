How we help

  1. Confirm what the notary needs

    The notary, lawyer or court on the other side sets the requirements. We confirm them before anything is signed.

  2. Translate and prepare

    Powers of attorney, identity and civil records, and contracts, prepared in both languages.

  3. Notarise and apostille in Australia

    Signing before an Australian Notary Public, then the DFAT apostille, in the order the destination country requires.

  4. Send it to the person acting for you

    The finished set goes to your representative, lawyer or notary, ready to use.

Common questions

Can you buy property in Europe without travelling from Australia?

Often, yes. In Italy, Spain, Greece and many other countries a representative can sign for you under a power of attorney that is notarised in Australia, apostilled by DFAT and translated. The receiving notary decides the exact form, so the wording is agreed with them first.

Where can you see property for sale or at auction?

On the official court and notarial auction portals and on the main listing sites for each country. The links on this page open those sources directly, so the listings are always current.

Do you sell property or recommend listings?

No. We are translators, not estate agents or lawyers. We prepare and coordinate the documents that let you buy, sell or inherit from Australia.
