Buying property in Spain from Australia, signed from home.

Australians buy homes in Spain every year, from city apartments in Valencia to island houses in Mallorca. With an NIE and an apostilled Spanish power of attorney, your representative signs at the notary for you, and we prepare every document in Spanish and English.

What you need

NIE

The Spanish foreigner identification number. It is the first document to arrange, and it is used for the purchase, taxes and utilities.

A Spanish bank account

Makes paying the deposit, the taxes and the bills simple once the home is yours.

A representative in Spain

A Spanish lawyer, a gestor or a trusted person who signs at the notary for you.

A poder notarial

The Spanish power of attorney, signed in Australia and apostilled by DFAT. Poder notarial en Australia

Buying in Spain, step by step

  1. Get your NIE

    The Número de Identidad de Extranjero is the Spanish foreigner identification number needed to buy, pay taxes and connect utilities. We prepare and translate the documents for the application.

  2. Agree the power of attorneyDone by us

    Your Spanish lawyer or the notary confirms the wording of the poder notarial. We prepare it in Spanish and English, so you know exactly what your representative is authorised to sign.

  3. Sign in Australia, we handle the apostilleDone by us

    We book the Australian Notary Public, usually in the same week, so you only need to attend and sign the poder notarial. We then take it to DFAT ourselves for the apostille, so it is valid in Spain.

  4. Reserve with the contrato de arras

    The deposit contract secures the property while the purchase is prepared. We translate it into English so every clause is clear before your representative signs.

  5. Your representative signs at the notary

    The escritura de compraventa, the purchase deed, is signed before the Spanish notario on your behalf. We translate the deed into English for your records.

  6. Register the property

    The deed is recorded at the Registro de la Propiedad, and the home is officially yours. We translate the registry extract, the nota simple, so you have it in English.

Common questions

Can you buy property in Spain from Australia?

Yes. You need an NIE (foreigner identification number), and a representative can sign for you at the notary under a Spanish power of attorney (poder notarial) signed before an Australian Notary Public and apostilled by DFAT.

Do you need to travel to Spain to buy?

No. With an apostilled poder notarial, your representative signs the contrato de arras and the escritura for you at the Spanish notary. Many buyers visit to choose the town and leave the signing to their representative.

What is an NIE and how do you get one from Australia?

The NIE is the Spanish foreigner identification number used to buy property, pay taxes and connect utilities. It can be requested through a Spanish consulate or by a representative in Spain, and we prepare and translate the supporting documents.

Where can you see property for sale in Spain?

The main listing sites are Idealista and Fotocasa. Court and state auctions are published on the Portal de Subastas of the Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE).

Which documents are translated for a Spanish purchase?

Typically the poder notarial and your identity documents, and on the Spanish side the contrato de arras, the escritura and the nota simple from the property registry, so you understand every document before it is signed.

Is Spanish your main language?

Yes. Spanish is our main language, so contracts, notary documents and correspondence with Spanish agents, lawyers and notaries are handled directly, without an intermediary.
