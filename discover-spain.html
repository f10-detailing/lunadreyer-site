Live the Spanish dream, not just the holiday.

Long summers, the Mediterranean on your doorstep, food and festivals all year, and a pace of life built around people. Spain is the country Australians fall for on holiday. These six places show why it is worth staying.

Six places to fall for

Extremadura

Mérida

Spain's best-preserved Roman city, founded as Augusta Emerita. Its archaeological ensemble is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the Roman theatre still hosts a classical theatre festival every summer. Quiet, affordable and an easy drive to Portugal.

Northern Spain

Asturias

Green mountains, a wild Atlantic coastline and the Picos de Europa National Park. Oviedo and Gijón offer city life, the fishing villages offer calm, and the cider houses offer one of Spain's most distinctive food cultures.

Andalusia

Málaga

The capital of the Costa del Sol and Picasso's birthplace, with the Museo Picasso, the Moorish Alcazaba and an international airport with direct links across Europe. Year-round sun with a real city behind the beaches.

Valencian Community

Valencia

Spain's third-largest city: the City of Arts and Sciences, long city beaches, the home of paella, and the Fallas festival, listed by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage. Big-city services at a calmer pace than Madrid or Barcelona.

Balearic Islands

Mallorca

Turquoise coves, the Serra de Tramuntana mountains and Palma's old town and cathedral on the bay. In Manacor, Rafa Nadal's home town, the Rafa Nadal Academy trains young players from around the world, a draw for tennis-loving families.

Murcia

La Manga del Mar Menor

A narrow strip of land more than 20 kilometres long between the Mediterranean and the Mar Menor, one of Europe's largest saltwater lagoons. Two seas, warm water and long summers, with sailing and water sports on the calm lagoon side.

Common questions

When is the best time to visit Spain?

Spring and autumn are ideal for exploring towns and cities, with warm days and fewer crowds. Summer is made for the coast and the islands, and the south and the Canary Islands stay mild right through winter.

Can you rent in Spain for a season first?

Yes. Seasonal rentals, known as alquiler de temporada, are a popular way to spend a few months in a town, live like a local and get to know the neighbourhoods before choosing where to settle.

Which part of Spain has the most sunshine?

The Mediterranean coast and Andalusia enjoy some of the sunniest weather in Europe, with the Costa del Sol, Murcia and the Valencian coast all famous for long, bright days for most of the year.
