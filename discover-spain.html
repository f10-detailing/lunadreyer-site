Property · Spain

Live the Spanish dream, not just the holiday.

Long summers, the Mediterranean on your doorstep, food and festivals all year, and a pace of life built around people. Spain is the country Australians fall for on holiday. These six places show why it is worth staying, and when you are ready to buy, we handle the Spanish paperwork from Australia.

Six places to fall for

Mérida

Extremadura. Spain's best-preserved Roman city, founded as Augusta Emerita. Its archaeological ensemble is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the Roman theatre still hosts a classical theatre festival every summer. Quiet, affordable and an easy drive to Portugal.

Asturias

Northern Spain. Green mountains, a wild Atlantic coastline and the Picos de Europa National Park. Oviedo and Gijón offer city life, the fishing villages offer calm, and the cider houses offer one of Spain's most distinctive food cultures.

Málaga

Andalusia. The capital of the Costa del Sol and Picasso's birthplace, with the Museo Picasso, the Moorish Alcazaba and an international airport with direct links across Europe. Year-round sun with a real city behind the beaches.

Valencia

Valencian Community. Spain's third-largest city: the City of Arts and Sciences, long city beaches, the home of paella, and the Fallas festival, listed by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage. Big-city services at a calmer pace than Madrid or Barcelona.

Mallorca

Balearic Islands. Turquoise coves, the Serra de Tramuntana mountains and Palma's old town and cathedral on the bay. In Manacor, Rafa Nadal's home town, the Rafa Nadal Academy trains young players from around the world, a draw for tennis-loving families.

La Manga del Mar Menor

Murcia. A narrow strip of land more than 20 kilometres long between the Mediterranean and the Mar Menor, one of Europe's largest saltwater lagoons. Two seas, warm water and long summers, with sailing and water sports on the calm lagoon side.

Buying in Spain from Australia

  1. Get your NIE

    The Spanish foreigner identification number needed to buy, pay taxes and connect utilities.

  2. Agree the power of attorney

    Your Spanish lawyer or the notary confirms the wording of the poder notarial. We prepare it in Spanish and English.

  3. Sign and apostille in Australia

    Signed before an Australian Notary Public, then apostilled by DFAT, so it is valid in Spain.

  4. Your representative signs at the notary

    The purchase deed is signed before the Spanish notario and then recorded at the Registro de la Propiedad.

Common questions

Can you buy property in Spain from Australia?

Yes. You need an NIE (foreigner identification number), and a representative can sign for you at the notary under a Spanish power of attorney (poder notarial) signed before an Australian Notary Public and apostilled by DFAT.

Where can you see property for sale in Spain?

The main listing sites are Idealista and Fotocasa. Court and state auctions are published on the Portal de Subastas of the Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE).

Is Spanish your main language?

Yes. Spanish is our main language, so contracts, notary documents and correspondence with Spanish agents and offices are handled directly, without an intermediary.
